Kelli has built a genuinely healthy, highly profitable business — ~59% net margins, $1.28 CPL, and elite 19:1 unit economics on a $5,045 LTV — almost entirely off four years of referrals from a PA mom group on Facebook. That referral engine is now tapping out, and the numbers show the consequence: she's bouncing between $35K and $48K months and panicking through the dips, when her goal is a predictable $50–60K every month. Section 03
Revenue Equilibrium

The leaky-bucket question: are you adding client value faster than churn is removing it? Revenue Equilibrium = LTV × New Clients Acquired per month. If that number is above your current revenue, you're building a compounding base. If it's roughly equal, you're treading water — replacing churn but not growing. This is the math underneath Kelli's "good month / panic month" cycle.

The core insight — this IS the "panic month" problem: At 7.2% churn on ~97 clients, Kelli loses roughly 7 clients a month. She closes about 7–8 a month. So she's running almost exactly in place — every new client basically just replaces a churned one. That's why revenue oscillates around the same band instead of climbing, and why a soft month (like April's 3 closes) immediately becomes a $34K dip. The entire fix is on one side of the equation: get new clients per month from ~8 up to 12–15. At 12–15 closes/month, equilibrium jumps to $60–76K and the base finally compounds — which is exactly the predictable $50–60K Kelli is asking for. New-client volume is a lead-gen problem, which is a paid-ads problem.

Section 05
Constraint Diagnosis

Fix these in order. Kelli's top constraints are all on the acquisition side — the delivery, margins, and economics are already healthy. Fixing #1 unlocks more than #3–#6 combined.

1
No Paid Acquisition Engine — 1 Entity, Referral Source Tapping Out
The business was built on ~4 years of referrals from a PA mom Facebook group — and that source is drying up. Meanwhile the paid account is a single ad pointed at a follower funnel. There is no DM-funnel entity portfolio replacing the referral engine. This is THE growth constraint: when the referral well runs dry and there's no paid machine behind it, revenue can only oscillate or decline.
Fix: Build the 10-entity DM-funnel portfolio from Section 04, led by the medical-pro identity (PA Origin Story, Nurse's Granola Bar) and the Busy Mom Food Guide lead-magnet activation. Target 8–10 distinct entities running simultaneously within 90 days.
2
Lead → Booked Call ~2% — The Setting Gap
Kelli generates 744–1,912 leads/month at ~$1 each, but only ~2% become booked calls. She has cycled through 6–8 setters (including a paid setter academy) and none stuck. The leads are cheap and plentiful; the conversion of lead → conversation → calendar is broken. Because her close rate is 56%, every additional booked call is worth a lot.
Fix: Stand up SetSmart (AI setter) — already in progress — and build a structured DM qualification → booking flow. Pair the new DM-funnel entities (comment-trigger CTAs) with SetSmart so the cheap volume actually converts to calls. Track lead→call weekly.
3
All Top-of-Funnel — No MOF Nurture Layer
Everything Kelli runs is cold, top-of-funnel content driving to a follow/DM. There is no middle-of-funnel layer that warms an interested lead before the call ask — no proof entities, no mechanism education, no objection-handling, no case studies in paid. Leads either convert immediately or evaporate. A warm audience is being generated every month and left completely unmined.
Fix: Build 3 MOF/BOF entities (22-Pounds Case Study, Three Night Shifts proof, Three Questions qualifier) and run them as retargeting against everyone who engaged with TOF content.
4
Show Rate Slipping — 62–64% in the Soft Months
Show rate was 79% in January, slipped to 62.5% in March and 63.6% in April, then recovered to 80% in May — below the 70% target on the down months. With low call volume, every no-show is expensive. The success coach has already delivered the show-rate optimization training; the leading indicator needs to be watched weekly, not monthly.
Fix: Implement the show-rate checklist + SMS-only confirmation sequence. Add a same-day confirmation touch. A warmer MOF layer (Constraint 3) compounds this.
5
Ad Account Massively Under-Funded — Marketing Margin ~3%
Kelli spends ~$1,200/month on ads against a business doing $35–48K/month at 59% net margin and 19:1 LTV:CAC. Marketing margin is ~3% when the healthy range is 10–20%. She doesn't trust ads yet because none have clearly worked — but that's a function of having one entity, not a function of the channel. The economics scream that she should be spending 5–10× more.
Fix: As the new entities prove out (kill rule: 2× CPL at 3 days), scale spend deliberately from ~$1.2K toward $5–6K/month. Max CAC headroom is enormous at a $5,045 LTV.
6
Data Hygiene — EOM Report Not Yet Trustworthy
The team just started using the client success dashboard / end-of-month report and isn't filling it out correctly yet, so CAC, LTV, and LTGP are only partially calculated (March is the only fully-populated month; February is blank entirely). You can't manage what you can't measure.
Fix: Lock a weekly + monthly data-entry SOP for the assistant coaches and VA. Get CAC/LTV/churn/equilibrium calculating cleanly every month.
✓
Margins, CPL, Close Rate & Unit Economics — Leave These Alone
59% net margin, $1.28 CPL, 56% close rate, and 19:1 LTV:CAC are all excellent and above IFCA targets. This is a healthy, profitable business. The temptation will be to "optimize" these — don't. The entire opportunity is on the acquisition side. Protect the margins and economics while you pour fuel on lead gen.

Section 05
Constraint Diagnosis

Fix these in order. Kelli's top constraints are all on the acquisition side — the delivery, margins, and economics are already healthy. Fixing #1 unlocks more than #3–#6 combined.

1
No Paid Acquisition Engine — 1 Entity, Referral Source Tapping Out
The business was built on ~4 years of referrals from a PA mom Facebook group — and that source is drying up. Meanwhile the paid account is a single ad pointed at a follower funnel. There is no DM-funnel entity portfolio replacing the referral engine. This is THE growth constraint: when the referral well runs dry and there's no paid machine behind it, revenue can only oscillate or decline.
Fix: Build the 10-entity DM-funnel portfolio from Section 04, led by the medical-pro identity (PA Origin Story, Nurse's Granola Bar) and the Busy Mom Food Guide lead-magnet activation. Target 8–10 distinct entities running simultaneously within 90 days.
2
Lead → Booked Call ~2% — The Setting Gap
Kelli generates 744–1,912 leads/month at ~$1 each, but only ~2% become booked calls. She has cycled through 6–8 setters (including a paid setter academy) and none stuck. The leads are cheap and plentiful; the conversion of lead → conversation → calendar is broken. Because her close rate is 56%, every additional booked call is worth a lot.
Fix: Stand up SetSmart (AI setter) — already in progress — and build a structured DM qualification → booking flow. Pair the new DM-funnel entities (comment-trigger CTAs) with SetSmart so the cheap volume actually converts to calls. Track lead→call weekly.
3
All Top-of-Funnel — No MOF Nurture Layer
Everything Kelli runs is cold, top-of-funnel content driving to a follow/DM. There is no middle-of-funnel layer that warms an interested lead before the call ask — no proof entities, no mechanism education, no objection-handling, no case studies in paid. Leads either convert immediately or evaporate. A warm audience is being generated every month and left completely unmined.
Fix: Build 3 MOF/BOF entities (22-Pounds Case Study, Three Night Shifts proof, Three Questions qualifier) and run them as retargeting against everyone who engaged with TOF content.
4
Show Rate Slipping — 62–64% in the Soft Months
Show rate was 79% in January, slipped to 62.5% in March and 63.6% in April, then recovered to 80% in May — below the 70% target on the down months. With low call volume, every no-show is expensive. The success coach has already delivered the show-rate optimization training; the leading indicator needs to be watched weekly, not monthly.
Fix: Implement the show-rate checklist + SMS-only confirmation sequence. Add a same-day confirmation touch. A warmer MOF layer (Constraint 3) compounds this.
5
Ad Account Massively Under-Funded — Marketing Margin ~3%
Kelli spends ~$1,200/month on ads against a business doing $35–48K/month at 59% net margin and 19:1 LTV:CAC. Marketing margin is ~3% when the healthy range is 10–20%. She doesn't trust ads yet because none have clearly worked — but that's a function of having one entity, not a function of the channel. The economics scream that she should be spending 5–10× more.
Fix: As the new entities prove out (kill rule: 2× CPL at 3 days), scale spend deliberately from ~$1.2K toward $5–6K/month. Max CAC headroom is enormous at a $5,045 LTV.
6
Data Hygiene — EOM Report Not Yet Trustworthy
The team just started using the client success dashboard / end-of-month report and isn't filling it out correctly yet, so CAC, LTV, and LTGP are only partially calculated (March is the only fully-populated month; February is blank entirely). You can't manage what you can't measure.
Fix: Lock a weekly + monthly data-entry SOP for the assistant coaches and VA. Get CAC/LTV/churn/equilibrium calculating cleanly every month.
✓
Margins, CPL, Close Rate & Unit Economics — Leave These Alone
59% net margin, $1.28 CPL, 56% close rate, and 19:1 LTV:CAC are all excellent and above IFCA targets. This is a healthy, profitable business. The temptation will be to "optimize" these — don't. The entire opportunity is on the acquisition side. Protect the margins and economics while you pour fuel on lead gen.
